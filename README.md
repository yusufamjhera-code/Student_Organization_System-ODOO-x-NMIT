## 🎯 The Challenge: Student Organization System
Building a single, unified software platform that serves as the digital backbone of a busy campus club. From running events and managing members to tracking finances, our system replaces disjointed spreadsheets, cash boxes, and WhatsApp groups with a single streamlined interface.

---

## ✨ Core Features & Solutions

### 🙋‍♂️ 1. Smart Member Management
* **The Problem:** Sign-ups were tracked in messy spreadsheets.
* **The Solution:** A centralized registration portal where the club can instantly see who a student is, their dues status, and their membership perks. Automated reminders ensure no one forgets to renew, and digital profiles make checking in at events instantaneous.

### 🎟️ 2. Seamless Event Ticketing (Spring Gala)
* **The Problem:** Tickets were sold at the door for cash, making attendance and revenue tracking impossible.
* **The Solution:** Online ticketing with dynamic pricing for members vs. non-members. Door staff can scan digital access tokens (QR codes) in seconds, eliminating paper lists. Post-event analytics provide instant reports on attendance and total revenue.

### 📢 3. Centralized Announcements
* **The Problem:** Updates were manually typed into multiple chaotic WhatsApp groups.
* **The Solution:** A built-in mailing list and simple announcement board. One message reaches the entire organization instantly, with a permanent record of what was communicated and when.

### 👕 4. Merchandise & Inventory (Hoodie Orders)
* **The Problem:** Manual size collection and cash chasing for club merchandise.
* **The Solution:** An online storefront allowing members to order hoodies from their phones 24/7. Real-time inventory tracking automatically subtracts stock, and digital payments eliminate the need to hunt down owed cash.

### 🍰 5. Volunteer Task Delegation (Bake Sale)
* **The Problem:** Event planning lived entirely in organizers' heads or scattered group chats.
* **The Solution:** A clear, visible task board. Anyone can see at a glance who is baking, who is buying supplies, and what still needs to be done, ensuring the fundraiser stays perfectly on track.

### 💰 6. Transparent Treasury & Finances
* **The Problem:** The treasurer spent hours at the end of the semester matching a physical notebook with a pile of receipts.
* **The Solution:** A comprehensive financial dashboard detailing dues collected, tickets/merchandise sold, and volunteer reimbursements. Every team member can understand the club's financial health at a glance.

## 🛠️ Tech Stack
* **Frontend:** HTML5, CSS3, Vanilla JavaScript
* **Backend / Database:** Firebase Authentication, Firestore
* **Email & Automation:** Google Apps Script
* **Hosting:** Netlify (with secure environment variable injection)
